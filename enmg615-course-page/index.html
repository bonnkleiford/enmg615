Course Overview

What this course is about

This course is intended to be an introduction to the fundamental models, methods, and applications of deterministic optimization. The focus will be on formulating and analyzing large-scale deterministic optimization models, which includes linear, integer, dynamic, and non-linear programs.

Course Learning Outcomes

What students will be able to do

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

  1. Recognize, understand, and discuss fundamental approaches, methodology, and real-world application areas of optimization in business and engineering.
  2. Formulate deterministic mathematical programs.
  3. Solve deterministic mathematical programs using optimization algorithms and perform sensitivity analysis.
  4. Implement and solve deterministic mathematical programs using a solver.
  5. Learn and practice communicating optimization projects effectively to various audiences.
